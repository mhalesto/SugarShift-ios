## Description (4000 char limit)

```
SugarShift is a bright, fast fruit match-3 with a simple promise: we respect
you.

OUR FAIR PLAY PROMISE
• Every one of the 200 levels is beatable without paying — we simulate and
  tune each board before it ships.
• No forced ads. No fake "1000 free spins" nonsense. What you see in our
  screenshots is the actual game.
• Made a mis-swap? Undo it. Yes, a match-3 with an undo button.
• Stuck on a level a few times? The game quietly helps instead of selling you
  the answer.

ONE BOARD. THE WHOLE WORLD.
Every day there is one Daily Board — seeded from the date, identical for every
player on the planet. Same fruit, same frost, same chocolate. Clear it, share
your emoji result card, and see where you land on the daily leaderboard. Your
streak builds rewards day after day, and the Home Screen widget keeps today's
board and your streak one glance away.

MATCH-3 WITH REAL TEETH
• 200 hand-tuned levels across six chapters, from Sweet Start to the Sugar
  Throne.
• Square-match a 2×2 to hatch a Fish — a seeker that hunts your goal tiles.
• Striped, wrapped, color bomb, and bomb specials that chain into huge
  cascades.
• Frost, locks, jelly, crates, color locks, vines, chests, spreading
  chocolate, rising syrup, and ticking fuses — introduced gently, combined
  wickedly.
• Boards shaped like donuts, diamonds, hourglasses, arrows, and crowns.
• Crown Challenges and level modifiers — 12-swap sprints, no-booster runs,
  double-banana scoring — for players who want mastery, not grind.

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• Lives refill on the lock screen: a Live Activity counts down your next heart
  in the Dynamic Island.
• Game Center leaderboards and achievements, including the daily board.
• Color-blind patterns, large text, reduced motion, high contrast, and
  VoiceOver-friendly fruit labels.
• Quick sessions, full offline play, and your progress syncs in the cloud.

No energy walls you have to pay through. No puzzle you can't solve. Just one
more sweet move.
```
